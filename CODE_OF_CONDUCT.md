# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The QUANTA2 project welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. We aim to maintain a respectful, accessible, and technically focused community.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, evidence, and technical decisions rather than individuals;
- provide actionable, evidence-based feedback;
- acknowledge uncertainty, correct mistakes, and give credit for contributions;
- respect privacy and avoid sharing personal information without permission; and
- keep discussions relevant to the project and its documented scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory conduct;
- personal attacks, insults, or repeated bad-faith disruption;
- publishing private or identifying information without consent;
- sexualized attention or unwelcome conduct;
- deliberately misleading technical claims or fabricated test evidence; and
- other conduct that would make a professional technical collaboration unsafe or hostile.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and other spaces where someone is representing the QUANTA2 project.

## Reporting a concern

To report conduct that violates this policy, contact the repository maintainer through the contact options on the [Vice13th GitHub profile](https://github.com/Vice13th). Include a link to the relevant discussion and a concise description. Do not post private or sensitive details in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed with appropriate care. The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Attribution

This project maintains its own concise participation policy. For additional background on open-source community standards, see the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
